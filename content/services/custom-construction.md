## Turn a broad idea into a sequence

Describe the result, the known constraints, and the order in which phases may need to happen. The contractor should explain what it can perform directly and which responsibilities belong to the owner or other qualified providers.

## Agree what the estimate covers

Before work is authorized, document assumptions, exclusions, dependencies, and how changes will be handled. This page is an example of the information to discuss; it is not a service promise or a quote.
