## Make the scope specific

“Clear the lot” can mean different things to different people. A useful page explains how the business defines the work area, handles existing material, and records exclusions before a project begins.

## Confirm the site requirements

Property permission, utility marking, local rules, and disposal requirements need project-specific review. The contractor and owner should agree who will verify each item; this sample makes no claim about a particular site or completed job.
