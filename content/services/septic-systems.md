## Start with what is known

Property boundaries, site access, existing records, and intended use can shape the first conversation. A real service page should explain what the contractor needs to review and which decisions require a qualified designer or local authority.

## Confirm responsibilities before work begins

Document who coordinates plans, permits, utility marking, installation, and inspection. Requirements differ by location and project, so this example does not promise that any particular approval or system is suitable.
