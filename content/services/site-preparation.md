## Describe the next phase

Site preparation may involve several kinds of work. A real contractor should name only the services it offers and explain how it confirms grading, drainage, access, and material needs for each property.

## Keep assumptions visible

Photos and a general location can help frame a conversation, but they do not replace a site review. Record what is known, what still needs investigation, and who is responsible for approvals before agreeing on scope or timing.
