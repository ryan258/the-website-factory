## Coordinate the work around the route

Plans should identify the route, connection points, known utility information, and the people responsible for each approval. A real business should describe its own role clearly and avoid implying that a line can be located or connected without the required checks.

## Set restoration expectations

Excavation can affect landscaping, drives, or other surfaces. Confirm what restoration is part of the agreed scope and what requires a separate plan before scheduling the work.
