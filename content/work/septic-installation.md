## Starting conditions

Describe the project context only after confirming what may be shared. Keep addresses private and explain which technical details came from approved plans or qualified professionals.

## Agreed scope

Name the work actually performed and who held each design, permit, installation, and inspection responsibility. Requirements differ by location, so a sample story must not be treated as technical guidance for another property.

## Outcome and follow-up

State verified completion facts and any follow-up that remained. Avoid making safety, code-compliance, or performance claims unless the owner can document and approve them.
