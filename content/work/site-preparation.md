## Starting conditions

The published version should describe the property and the challenge in terms the owner has approved. Include useful context such as access, slope, drainage, or the planned next phase only when those details have been verified.

## Agreed scope

Explain what the contractor was asked to do, which work was included, and which decisions or approvals remained with others. Avoid implying a result that the project record does not support.

## Outcome and follow-up

Record the completed work and any limits or remaining steps. Use before-and-after photographs only when they show the same project accurately and the owner has permission to publish them.
