# Market landscape and SWOT

Research snapshot · November 12, 2026 · Internal planning

## Position to test

Lead with **a website design and build service for small service businesses that need customers to understand their offer and make contact**. Use 258 Web Co. as the customer-facing studio and The Website Factory as the name of the production toolkit. The studio can describe what the toolkit makes possible in client terms, including a reviewed page plan, a site shaped around the business, and a handoff with build and update instructions. The repository documents this division. The audience and offer are positioning hypotheses. [Agency workflow](../agency-workflow.md) · [Project direction](../../roadmap.md) · [Selected storefront](../../data/factory.json)

Bentonville and Northwest Arkansas are the current storefront's configured geography and a reasonable starting point for audience research, subject to confirming service coverage. The best first test is an owner-operated trade or local service business with a short service list, an existing business identity, and a clear call or enquiry path. Website replacements where the domain, hosting or update responsibilities have become unclear are a second use case. Independent developers and small studios are a potential future audience for a separate operator-facing offer. Interest, onboarding effort, and support costs for that offer remain unmeasured. [Storefront identity](../../data/site.yaml) · [Client delivery cycle](../agency-workflow.md)

| Audience | Trigger | Decision factors | Proof to show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner of a local service business | Old or missing website, confusing service descriptions, customers struggling to reach the business | Clear scope, predictable responsibilities, believable examples, update path, reliable enquiries | A labeled example with service and contact journeys, a plain-language scope, and a sample handoff |
| Owner replacing an agency or DIY site | Difficulty making updates, unclear account ownership, upcoming redesign | What moves, what gets rebuilt, redirects, who owns accounts, and how ongoing support works | An inventory of deliverables, account responsibilities, a sample redirect map, and one verified rebuild |
| Independent builder or small studio | Repeated setup and review work across similar brochure sites | Setup and training effort, source control, repeatable checks, delivery flexibility, and maintenance | A clean-machine run from brief to client copy and a second developer's handoff exercise |

Lead qualification matters more than broad category coverage. A brochure site with a small set of service pages, occasional updates, and an agreed contact path fits the core workflow. Frequent visual editing by several staff, complex e-commerce, authenticated member experiences, or specialist integrations call for a different scope and possibly a different platform. A starter label such as “clinic” describes a composition. Healthcare intake, privacy requirements, and platform suitability need project-specific review. ## SWOT

### Strengths

| Factor | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Review is part of the workflow.** The planner separates draft from approved copy. Schema and output checks test site structure. A rules-based claims scan surfaces patterns that require human confirmation, and the release workflow requires current content review. [Agency workflow](../agency-workflow.md) · [Claims scanner](../../scripts/claims.py) · [Release instructions](../../HAPPY-PATH.md) | Demonstrate the moment when a missing business fact is surfaced and resolved. Describe a review process with clear responsibilities. Claim detection covers defined patterns, so a person must still verify business facts. |
| **A separate source handoff is supported.** Client sites receive isolated source. The eject command is designed to retain a plain Hugo copy only if its build matches the reference output. [Source-copy workflow](../../README.md#shared-scaffold-ownership) · [Handoff command](../../scripts/eject.py) | Show a sample file inventory, who can update it, and how to rebuild. Explain the separate work needed to move or replace enquiry processing. |
| **A substantial library supports variation.** The repository documents 39 module families with three layouts each and presets for service businesses, trades, and other use cases. [Component library](../component-library.md) · [Recorded acceptance](../acceptance.md) | Use the closest starter to focus the brief. Show how its sections, copy, and visual style can be changed for a real audience. |
| **Planning fits a local, single-operator workflow.** The workshop supports local saves, JSON backup and restore, a Markdown design brief, and plan compilation. [Workshop workflow](../agency-workflow.md) | Preserve a short path to resume work and a portable backup. Explain the browser-origin storage behavior in operator material. |

### Weaknesses

| Factor | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Commercial proof is thin in the reviewed sources.** The example projects are fictional. The acceptance record describes specific local checks and lists external and manual verification gaps. [Project direction](../../README.md#direction-and-current-state) · [Recorded acceptance](../acceptance.md) | Use labeled examples and check records. Claims about results, satisfaction, lead generation, or time savings need a separate evidence record. |
| **Delivery takes technical ownership.** Toolchain setup, project files, builds, and exports have operator steps. Local storage is scoped to a browser origin. There is no client-facing runtime CMS in the current operating model. [Tooling and setup](../../README.md) · [Workshop workflow](../agency-workflow.md) · [Technical direction](../../roadmap.md) | Establish who will update and rebuild the site. Estimate support and backups as part of a project. Check whether the handoff is understandable to someone who has never run the Factory. |
| **Launch and enquiry receipt depend on configuration.** The current storefront source has indexing disabled and its form off. The Pages recipe needs explicit release and enquiry setup. [Hugo configuration](../../hugo.toml) · [Release instructions](../../HAPPY-PATH.md) | Use the storefront and any launch CTA as drafts until the operator verifies a working public contact path and actual receipt. Configuration alone establishes neither live state nor delivery. |
| **Factory language can obscure the service.** “Factory” describes internal reuse. A customer may hear mass production, especially if the pitch leads with layout counts or AI. [Agency workflow](../agency-workflow.md) | Use the studio name and business outcome in prospect materials. Test whether prospective clients can explain the service and the handoff in their own words. |

| Factor | Implication |
| --- | --- |

[Matchstick Studio offer](https://matchstickstudio.co/small-business-websites/) · [Bentonville Web Design offer](https://bentonville-webdesign.com/) | Show the total responsibilities and line items on both sides of the handoff. ## What would change the strategy

- **First customer conversations.** Ask three to five qualified service owners what changed, which alternatives they considered, who edits their site, and what they expect to receive. Show the example after hearing their priorities. Record the phrases they use and the objections that repeat. This is qualitative discovery with a small sample.
- **First delivery.** Record elapsed days and active hours separately. Capture time waiting for content or approval, revision causes, setup, verification, and post-handoff support. Compare time savings only against a documented baseline with similar scope.
- **Channel fit.** When an authorized launch is ready, record referral source, enquiry, fit, proposal, and accepted project. The first few qualified enquiries can reveal where the pitch is unclear. Growth projections and conversion benchmarks need more evidence.
- **Positioning decision.** If owners mainly value frequent self-editing and bundled marketing, tighten lead qualification and clarify the site update agreement. If owners repeatedly value a straightforward handoff and can explain it after seeing the sample, give it more space in the pitch. If an external builder can run the workflow with little intervention, consider a separate operator guide or pilot offer.

Source-supported findings cover the repository's documented architecture and the alternatives' published capabilities. The relative importance of buying criteria, viability of the proposed service scope, and demand for an external toolkit are hypotheses. Client outcomes, willingness to pay, actual public release and intake, and current project-wide accessibility and performance are unverified in this research. Recheck vendor capabilities and competitor offers before using any comparison in public materials.
