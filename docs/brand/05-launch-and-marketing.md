# Launch and marketing playbook

## First campaign

**Audience**\
Owners of local service businesses who have an outdated site, an upcoming launch, or a service customers keep asking them to explain.

**Message**\
A clearer website for your service business.

**Offer**\
A scoped website build that explains services, coverage, and the next step. Use a focused landing page or a redesign as a variation when the need is narrower. Discuss hosting, changes, and care separately in the quote. Lead with one offer. Confirm geography and capacity before promoting availability.

**Proof**\
Use the labeled [Good Neighbor demo and sample handoff](collateral/good-neighbor-demo.md) to show the customer journey. Share the [studio one-sheet PDF](collateral/258webco-one-sheet.pdf) in direct introductions. Keep completed-client stories and measured results for work with documented permission and evidence.

**Next action**\
“Start a project” once a contact path is ready. During a private preview, use “See the website example” or invite a reply in an existing email thread.

## Channel plan

| Priority | Channel | Asset and action | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warm referrals | Send the one-sheet with a short, relevant introduction. Point to one example that matches the recipient’s business. | Referral source, questions asked, qualified conversations |
| 2 | Studio site | Lead with the core offer. Show the visitor path in a labeled sample. Describe scope and handoff on Services. Give Contact one tested route. | Enquiries with a source or referring link, project fit, common objections |
| 3 | Useful search content | Create one substantive page per real service or buying question. Use specific page titles, ordinary customer language, clear internal links, and approved business facts. | Search impressions and queries if the owner connects Search Console, relevant enquiries |
| 4 | Organic social | Post an annotated example, a review checklist, and a clear explanation of what a handoff includes. Reuse one image with a different caption for each purpose. | Replies, referral mentions, prospects who viewed the example |
| 5 | Studio partner conversations | Share a technical walkthrough privately with a designer or developer evaluating a specific engagement. Agree on roles and scope before offering white-label work. | Handoff questions, build and update effort, support and rights requirements |

Search pages should reflect services and areas the studio can confirm. Use a page title and main heading that describe the actual page, and give links meaningful text. Confirm indexing and submit the sitemap only when the public release is ready. Evaluate Google Business Profile only if the studio qualifies under its current in-person contact rules. [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials), [Google Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13762416#eligibility)

## Four-week first pass

| Week | Deliverable | Decision or next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Choose the lead offer, build a clean fictional example from the local-service preset, and assemble a sample handoff. | Confirm the target geography, who can take enquiries, and the exact offer. |
| 2 | Show the one-sheet and example privately to a few people who resemble the intended buyers. Ask them to describe the offer and find the next action. | Revise the phrase or example that caused the same confusion twice. |
| 3 | Prepare the storefront copy. Run the project’s review and release checks and confirm the real contact path when authorized. | Owner chooses whether to launch. Prepare introductions once the recipient and route are known. |
| 4 | Review the first conversations and follow-up effort. Publish one useful answer to a question buyers actually asked if approved. | Repeat the channel that yielded a relevant conversation at manageable effort. |

If launch authorization or contact readiness is pending, continue with private examples and feedback. Budget paid promotion separately after the offer and response path have produced evidence. Treat the weeks as a sequence the owner can resume at any point.

## Measurement

Keep a private log of dates, sources, project fit, next steps, and effort in the smallest format the operator will keep current. Start with an optional “How did you hear about us?” question or record the source from the conversation. Add consented tracking only if a specific question justifies it.

| Metric | How to count it | What to do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified enquiries | People with a real project, useful scope fit, and a feasible next conversation | Adjust audience or lead message if enquiries repeatedly miss the offer |
| Proposal rate | Proposals sent ÷ qualified enquiries in the same tracked cohort | Review what stalls after discovery |
| Win rate | Accepted proposals ÷ decided proposals | Record lost reasons and use counts alongside the rate |
| Delivery effort | Active time by brief, content, build, review, and handoff | Use actual effort to evaluate pricing and identify repeatable work |
| Handoff completion | Projects with accepted files, access, instructions, and named update owner ÷ delivered projects | Resolve the most common missing step |
| Early support burden | Post-handoff requests and effort during the agreed follow-up window | Clarify scope or improve instructions where the same request recurs |

Use “no data” for an empty denominator. Compare the same offer and cohort over time. For a first pilot, record evidence and buyer language before setting targets or promising a turnaround.
