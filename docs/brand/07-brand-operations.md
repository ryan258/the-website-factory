| Change | Edit here | Review here |
| --- | --- | --- |

Keep brand source files with their documented exports. Factory materials and studio campaign drafts live under `docs/brand/`. The workshop uses separate local output. Use the [operator guide](../operator-guide.md) to resume the build workflow.

## Claim decisions

Keep the exact line, where it appears, the source, the date, who confirmed it, and any reason to revisit it. One small private record per claim is enough. The studio’s preset review is the release record for its content, and the dated acceptance report is the record of what was tested.

| Claim | Evidence to retain before use |
| --- | --- |
| Studio name, locality, service area, contact route | Owner confirmation of exact details and a tested contact path |
| Price, turnaround, availability, support terms | Owner’s decision, scope and date, and the applicable offer terms |
| Performance figure or comparative result | Specific pages and release, dated method and conditions, and a comparable baseline for a comparison |
| Accessibility statement | Named criteria or behaviors and the extent of automated and manual testing for the delivered output |
| Client result, logo, or quote | Original evidence, approved wording and attribution, scope, and permission to share |
| Screenshot, photo, typeface, or illustration | Origin, license or permission, allowed uses, and required credits |
| Source delivery and future changes | Agreed deliverable, usage terms, named account owners, build and update instructions |

The current preset includes fixed speed-related figures and pricing references. Review each in context before reuse. A previously approved content hash establishes which text was reviewed. A current measurement or business confirmation establishes whether that claim can still be used. When evidence expires or scope changes, update or remove the copy, review again, and regenerate derived assets.

## Release checklist

1. **Confirm the offer.** Verify audience, service area, availability, page scope, proposed fee terms, and who can handle enquiries and support.
2. **Review the content.** Confirm business details, every claim, and every asset’s permitted use. Label demos wherever they appear. Replace or omit unknowns before a public release.
3. **Review the destination.** Check the intended contact path, the promise made near it, and who will reply. If intake is pending, keep materials suitable for private sharing and replies in an existing thread.
4. **Check the output.** Review copy, headings, keyboard flow, zoom, contrast, images, titles, social crop, links, relevant generated output, and the agreed visitor tasks. Record what was checked.
5. **Record the release decision.** Use the project’s [release checklist](../../HAPPY-PATH.md) and the [operator guide](../operator-guide.md) for content approval and release settings. The owner decides when and where to publish. Verify real receipt when the owner authorizes live intake.
6. **Save the next step.** Keep a dated note with the released assets, tested paths, approved claims, and next update. Revisit claims whenever an offer, release, or measured result changes.

> **Use and audience** [Private preview, sales use, or planned public placement]  
> **Decision** [Approved use or changes needed]  
> **Open items** [Fact, right, check, or dependency and its owner]

Review the proposed Factory name and mark for clearance before public trademark use. Set usage and distribution terms before offering the toolkit or its source to third parties.
