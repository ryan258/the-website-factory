## Show the workflow locally

From the factory folder:

```sh
./wf open --workshop
```

Open **Living Style Guide** for actual visual foundations, components, and usage examples. Open the **Good Neighbor** local-service preview at `/site-kit/local-service/` for a labeled fictional site. Walk through the service cards, service area, and sample contact path. The [client handoff guide](../operator-guide.md#final-delivery-without-the-factory) shows what a source delivery can contain and how to verify it.

## First campaign decision

Choose one real local-service audience and show them the studio one-sheet and the labeled Good Neighbor preview. Ask what they think they would receive at handoff and where they would go to start. Use the answers to sharpen the pitch, then confirm the offer, geography, and contact path before public use.

This kit proposes a studio campaign and a distinct Factory identity for owner review. The operating workflow and brand evidence are documented alongside the proposals.
