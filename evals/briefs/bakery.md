# Brief: Rise & Crumb Bakery (fictional test brief)

Rise & Crumb is a small neighborhood bakery run by two owners. They bake sourdough
bread, pastries, and celebration cakes. Cakes must be ordered at least 3 days ahead.

They want a simple website so people can see what they sell, understand how to order
a cake, and get in touch. Most customers live nearby and visit on foot.

They have not decided their opening hours for the new season yet. They do not want
prices on the website. They have no photos ready.
