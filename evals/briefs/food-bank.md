# Brief: Maple Street Food Bank (fictional test brief)

Maple Street Food Bank is a volunteer-run food bank. People can collect groceries
once a week with no paperwork. Volunteers sort donations and run the collection day.

The website should explain how to get food, how to volunteer, and how to donate food.
They do not take money donations online. The collection day and time are changing and
are not confirmed. They have no statistics about how many people they help and do not
want any numbers on the site.
