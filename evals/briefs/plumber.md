# Brief: Northside Plumbing (fictional test brief)

Northside Plumbing is a two-person plumbing business. They fix leaks, replace water
heaters, and unblock drains for homes. They also do small bathroom refits.

They want customers to understand what they do, what happens on a first visit, and
how to book. They do emergency call-outs but have not agreed their emergency hours.
They are applying for a trade certification but do not have it yet, so it must not be
mentioned. They serve the north side of town; the exact neighborhoods are not decided.
