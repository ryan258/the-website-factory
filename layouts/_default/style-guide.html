Page Assembly

Choose the closest starter composition, then keep the sections that help the visitor make a decision. Use the starter previews to see how the pieces work together.

Homepage
Lead with who the business helps, follow with services and approved work, and give visitors one useful next step.
Service page
Explain fit, scope, and the process. Use a comparison when differences matter and answer genuine objections in questions.
Contact page
Show the confirmed contact route and say what happens after someone acts. Match the explanation to the real delivery configuration.

Section order and variant keys live under pages in the preset. Each page section points to a content object under the top-level sections key. Use one lead heading and give each destination a stable anchor.
